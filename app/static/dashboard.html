Presets

Ready-made strategies. They hold only the rule choices. Every number is left empty for you to key in. Load fills the Strategy Hub for the pair you chose; nothing is saved on a pair until you press Save or Execute.

Loss limits

Daily and weekly limits, for one account and for all accounts together. Each limit is an amount in USDT or a % of the account balance at the start of the day (or week); you can key in both and the first one reached counts. When the closed result of the day (or week) is down by the limit, the Strategy Hub pairs in that scope make no new entry until the period is over, and you get an email once. A trade that is already open is never touched. Empty = no limit. A day starts at 00:00 Singapore time, a week on Monday at 00:00. The limit of one strategy is in its own Loss limit tab.
