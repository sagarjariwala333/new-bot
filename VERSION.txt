strategy_hub_bot - CLEAN HUB BUILD (owner only)
Built: 2026-10-24 SGT (see the zip file name for the time).

WHAT IT IS: one bot where YOU build the strategy per pair in the Strategy Hub tab, from boxes you tick. Nothing is preset.
- First install is fully BLANK: no starter values. The setup page holds only pair-level settings (candle timeframe, isolated margin, poll seconds, Telegram, price-feed stale seconds, OKX trigger type). Fill them and press Confirm.
- A pair with NO saved plan will NOT start.
- Hub screen = LAYOUT 2 (2026-10-23): strategy name + account at the top, menu of 8 functions on the left (Pair & chart, Indicators, Combine & flip + hold rule, Entry, Max loss, Stop loss (trailing), Take profit, Shadow mode), the boxes of the open function on the right with a short explanation beside each, Save and Execute at the bottom. The first 4 functions are needed by every strategy; the other 4 are picked. Execute = save + start the pair (after a confirm).
- Flip = CLOSE, FLAT, CHECK FOR CHANGES IN RULES, ELSE OPEN OPPOSITE IF NO CHANGE.
- Candle colour only for Heikin Ashi / Renko. Signals from HA/Renko, trades on normal candles.
- The old Hull fields are gone from the setup page and the Add pair form. The strategy lives only in the Strategy Hub boxes. (The old Hull code is still in the files, unreachable.)

SHADOW MODE in the Hub (new): after a real trade closes in profit and the tracker reaches a new high, the next N trades are PAPER only, following the plan's entry rules, wait, flip / hold, size and max-loss stop. Paper trades do NOT simulate take profit or the trailing stop. The tracker follows every real result, even when the box is not ticked; turning the box off ends a running shadow period.
FIXED BASE AMOUNT (new, 2026-10-23): ENTRY SIZE has a method choice. 'Allocation % of balance' works as before. 'Fixed base amount (grows with the last closed total)': you key in the base in USDT (margin; leverage stays in its own box; the position is amount x leverage). First trade = the base. After each FULL close the next entry = the total of that trade (its entry amount + every fill added up: TP1/TP2/TP3, SL, trailing, BE, net of fees), never below the base. Trend and against-the-trend each have their own base; ONE running total for both. No cap, no reset. The running total is saved on the server and survives a restart. Paper (shadow) trades never change it.
MAX LOSS (new own box, 2026-10-23): choose what the loss is measured on: % of available balance (as before), % of entry amount (the margin before leverage), or a fixed $ amount. Older saved plans keep working as '% of available balance' and 'Allocation %'.
AT LEAST N (new, 2026-10-23): ENTRY and FLIP / EXIT combine each have ALL, ANY or 'At least N' (you key in N; N cannot be more than the indicators ticked on that side).
LOCK-OUT % (new, 2026-10-23, fixed base only, optional): next entry = the entry just closed + that % of the trade's profit; a loss comes off in full; the base is still the minimum. Empty = off (the whole total is carried).
EMAIL ALERT ON MARGIN (new, 2026-10-23): when an entry needs more margin than the available balance at that moment, an email (and Telegram) is sent, once per candle. It never blocks or changes the entry. Needs the SMTP settings and the alert email.
ENTRY WAIT WARNING: the dashboard pops up 'Delay in entry up to N candles' when you set Entry wait above 0. Default stays 0 (no delay).
STRATEGY NAME: a label saved in the plan (renaming it does not count as a rule change).
TREND / COUNTER-TREND SIZING in the Hub (new): chart close vs an EMA you choose decides which size (allocation + leverage) an entry uses. Off = one size.
NOT PROVEN on a real exchange: TP limit orders, trailing-stop moves (use Binance testnet / OKX demo first).
Order-id prefixes ('hullbot_' / 'hullbotokx') are unchanged on purpose, so orders already on an exchange are still recognised.
Old build notes in docs/ keep their old names (history only).
Handoff for a new chat: docs/HANDOFF_READ_FIRST.md
INFO ICONS (new, 2026-10-23): an "i" beside every field; click it for the full explanation pop-up. Texts: HELP table in app/static/dashboard.html. Check: tests/browser_info_icons_check.py.
STOP LADDER (new, 2026-10-23): Max loss (last line of defense), Stop Loss (new), Trailing Stop (Step / lowest low-highest high / moving average), Take profit in % of ATR or % of price movement, Allow Mid-Trend Re-entry (off by default). Nearest stop on the exchange (stop-limit for Stop Loss / Trailing Stop, stop-market for Max loss); the others watched live by the bot. NOT PROVEN on a real exchange - test on testnet / demo first. KIV list: docs/HANDOFF_READ_FIRST.md.

TRAILING STOP 4th TYPE (2026-10-28): 'Signal candle high / low +/- ATR'. Long: signal candle low minus the distance; short: signal candle high plus it. Distance (% of ATR, 100 = 1 ATR, starts at 100, editable) uses the ATR of the signal candle, fixed for the trade. At every candle close the level is worked out again from the candle that just closed and only ever moves nearer (into profit), never further from the entry. It rests on the exchange like the other trailing stops; the live price through it closes the trade. Stop Loss and Max Loss stay behind it. Same in the shadow, Analysis and the Pine script (the Pine script is not compiled here). Nothing removed. Tests: tests/test_hub_trail_candle.py, tests/browser_trail_candle_check.py.

RSI CROSSES ITS OWN MA (2026-11-01): RSI has a 4th condition 'RSI crosses its own MA'. Long = the RSI line crosses UP through its own moving average, short = it crosses DOWN through it (the average is taken of the RSI line, not of the price). Average type: SMA, EMA, WMA, HMA or ALMA (ALMA also has Offset and Sigma). You key in the average length and N (signal stays on); the RSI Length is the RSI's own. Nothing is filled in for you, not in the presets. Can be ticked together with the other RSI conditions (all ticked must agree). Also in the Pine script (STRATEGY_HUB_BLANK.pine, RSI group). LOSS LIMIT IN % (2026-11-02): every Loss limit (this strategy, one account, all accounts together; daily and weekly) can now be a USDT amount OR a % of the account balance at the start of that day / week (you can key in both: the first one reached stops the entries). The bot saves the account balance at the first check after 00:00 SGT (Monday 00:00 for the week); the % is worked out once from it, so the limit stays the same USDT number all day. If the bot sees the account late in the period, the balance saved is the balance then minus what the ledger shows as closed since the start. For 'all accounts together' the % is taken of the saved balances of the accounts added up (an account with no Strategy Hub pair running saves none). Only used when a % is keyed in; it reads the account balance (read only), never touches a trade. No numbers by default. Tests: tests/test_hub_loss_limit_pct.py (50), tests/browser_loss_cooldown_check.py (49). Not proven on a real exchange.

EXIT WHEN + BOLLINGER + SUPERTREND (2026-11-04): (1) New optional function 'Exit when' (last in the menu, OFF unless picked). Signal: 'Both MA 1 and MA 2 curve against the trade' or 'MA 2 only (the slower one) curves against the trade'. Curving against = the MA value after the candle close is lower than the one before (long) or higher (short). After the close: 'Close and wait for a fresh signal' (flat; the same side stays blocked until its entry signal has ended, even if Allow Mid-Trend Re-entry is on) or 'Close and flip to the other side' (uses the flip wait). A flip signal on the same candle goes first (one flip, not two). It works with ANY plan (not only MA Crossover) and needs the MA 2 numbers (and MA 1 for 'Both'). The Hold rule does not stop Exit when. (2) Candle colour: new option 'Previous candle was the opposite colour' (OFF if absent). (3) New indicator 'Bollinger Bands' (length, multiplier, N): long = candle LOW at or under the lower band within N candles, short = HIGH at or over the upper band; middle line = SMA of the chart close, population stdev. CTREND tick 'Bollinger half': long in the lower half (close below the middle), short in the upper half is WITH the trend; anything else, including exactly on the middle line, is counter (smaller size). (4) New indicator 'Supertrend' (ATR length, factor, conditions 'Trend direction' / 'Fresh flip within N candles'), built like Pine's ta.supertrend (hl2, RMA ATR) on the chart candles. (5) Trailing Stop 5th type 'Supertrend line': at every candle close the stop is set at the line of the candle that closed; only tightens; set at entry if the line is on the protective side; a line on the wrong side does nothing and does not close the trade. Needs only the Supertrend ATR length and factor. No numbers or defaults added anywhere; old plans and old saved states load unchanged. Shadow, Analysis (backtest) and the Pine script mirror all of it (the Pine script is NOT compiled here). Tests: tests/test_hub_exit_bb_sup.py (60), tests/test_pine_text.py (5), tests/browser_exit_bb_sup_check.py (37); 9 deliberate-break checks all caught. Not proven on a real exchange. NOT built (parked): RSI with Bollinger, ADX-flip changes, Hedge Mode.

CHANGE AN OPEN TRADE (2026-11-05, owner: "Yes do that.. go"; "MUST have both options"): when you press Save or Execute while a trade is open on the pair, the review box shows two choices and NOTHING is pre-selected (Confirm stays off until you choose): 'Apply to the open trade now' or 'Apply from the next trade'. If 'now': a second choice 'Only tighter' or 'Allow looser too' (none pre-selected). 'Next trade' = the old behaviour (the open trade keeps its rules, the plan starts when flat). 'Now' changes ONLY: Trailing Stop, Stop Loss, Max Loss per Trade, Break-Even, Take profit, Exit when, Hold rule ADX level. Everything else you changed (entry signals + indicators, flip rules, Indicator Combination, size, leverage, chart, timeframe) is saved and starts from the next trade. The new stop levels are worked out as if the new setting had been on at the entry (signal candle, entry price, entry amount). Only tighter: a stop moves only if it protects more; a stop you turned off is NOT taken away; the answer lists what was kept. Allow looser too: the new level is used as it is, a stop turned off is removed. A changed Take profit cancels the resting Take profit order on the exchange and places the new level (already filled levels stay). The stop on the exchange is moved by the bot (new order first, then the old one is cancelled). After Save the dashboard shows what was changed, what was NOT changed and what waits. If the change fails, the open trade keeps its rules and the plan is still saved for the next trade. Tests: tests/test_hub_amend_open_trade.py (27), tests/browser_amend_check.py (19); 13 deliberate-break checks all caught. Not proven on a real exchange (Binance testnet first; OKX untested).

SUPPORT / RESISTANCE (2026-11-05, owner: "go"): new function and indicator row 'Support / Resistance'. The chart decides the range (highest/lowest of N candles, swing points, nearest tops/bottoms, flat or diagonal). Entries: bounce, breakout, retest (long/short ticks, zone and stop as % of ATR). Exits: other end, middle line, range breaks, close back inside. What ends a retest wait: level fails / new range / price runs away / nothing. Same-candle conflict = flat, lock the broken level, wait for the retest. Can be its own strategy or one more block in the Indicators list. Chart tab shows lines, B/K/R badges and a table. Nothing is preselected and no numbers are preset. Pine script does not include it. Not proven on a real exchange.
